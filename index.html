About BELTTOA

Legitimate local operators, one shared standard

Bacuit El Nido Travel and Tour Operators Association, Inc. (BELTTOA) is the LGU's solely accredited travel agency association in El Nido, Palawan.

Our story

BELTTOA was established in 2018 by a group of travel agency owners, initially starting with approximately 80 members.

Our primary objective was to secure accreditation from the Local Government Unit (LGU) to ensure our legitimacy. We aimed to establish a shared goal: not only to boost El Nido's tourism but also to standardize island hopping tours and regulate their pricing.

When you book with an accredited BELTTOA member, you support local operators who follow the town's tourism rules and standard tour pricing.

Mission

To unite and empower El Nido's travel agencies by securing official Local Government Unit (LGU) accreditation, ensuring strict regulatory compliance, and establishing uniform standards and fair pricing for island hopping tours. We are dedicated to boosting El Nido tourism sustainably, protecting the interests of our approximately 80 founding members, and delivering legitimate, exceptional travel experiences to the global public.

Vision

To be the premier, government-accredited association of legitimate travel agencies in El Nido, fostering a sustainable, unified, and world-class tourism industry where local businesses thrive under shared standards, fair pricing, and a collective passion for our community.

Main Goal

To establish a fully accredited, unified network of legitimate travel agencies in El Nido that ensures fair business practices, standardized tour operations, and sustainable local tourism growth.

Working with the Municipality of El Nido

BELTTOA conceived the "Seven (7) Wonders of El Nido," a rebranding of the island tours designed to address carrying capacity at popular destinations.

During the pandemic, BELTTOA requested the tour rate adjustments that became part of Ordinance No. 024, Series of 2020, approved by the Sangguniang Bayan on October 26, 2020.

BELTTOA in action

Clean-ups, trainings, travel expos and community events with the LGU of El Nido.

Before You Go

Plan your El Nido trip

A quick guide to fees, lagoon rules, island tours and getting here. Your accredited BELTTOA member can help with everything below when you book.

Lagoon rules

  • Big Lagoon and Small Lagoon allow a limited number of visitors per time slot, so your agency books your slot in advance.
  • Motorized boats cannot enter the lagoons. You kayak or swim in from the mouth.
  • Joiner tours do not visit Big Lagoon and Small Lagoon on the same day.

Getting to El Nido

  • By air: flights to Lio Airport leave from Clark, with connections from Cebu, Caticlan, Bohol and Coron. There are no direct flights from Manila since March 29, 2026.
  • From Puerto Princesa: about 5 to 6 hours by bus or shared van, ending at the El Nido Transport Terminal in Corong Corong.
  • From Coron: fast ferry, about 3.5 to 4 hours.

Best time to visit

  • Calmest seas: February and March.
  • Wet season: July to October. Tours are cancelled more often, so keep a spare day in your plans.
  • Lean season: fewer crowds, plus ₱300 OFF with accredited BELTTOA members from November 15 to December 15, 2026.
